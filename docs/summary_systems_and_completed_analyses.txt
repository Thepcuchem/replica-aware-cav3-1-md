5. PROLIF PROTEIN-LIGAND INTERACTIONS
-------------------------------------

Completed for Z944 and mZ944 runs 1-3.

Major conclusions:
  - Phe956 is a persistent interaction in both systems.
  - Z944 frequently involves Phe956, Asn952, Leu920, and Thr921.
  - mZ944 retains a recurring Thr921-Phe956 interaction core.
  - Lys1462 becomes particularly important in selected replicas.
  - Domain II forms the principal conserved interaction hotspot.
  - Redistribution toward Domain III is replica-dependent.

Three-replica alluvial comparison:
  /work1/ted/June2023/WithLigand/analysis_replicas/prolif/Z944_vs_mZ944_alluvial

9. LIGAND CONFORMATIONAL DYNAMICS
---------------------------------

Seven ligand dihedrals were analyzed for Z944 and mZ944 runs 1-3
over the final 300 ns:

chi1: C9-N3-C8-C7
chi2: N3-C8-C7-N1
chi3: C8-C7-N1-C4
chi4: C3-C1-C6-N2
chi5: C1-C6-N2-C10
chi6: C6-N2-C10-C14
chi7: N2-C10-C14-C16

Main findings:
  - chi1 and chi6 remain tightly centered near the trans boundary.
  - chi2 is the most heterogeneous and replica-dependent torsion.
  - chi3 shifts from approximately 121 degrees in Z944 to 100
    degrees in mZ944.
  - Z944 has greater chi4 and chi5 variability.
  - mZ944 has greater chi7 variability, driven by alternative states
    in runs 1 and 3.

Timelines, pooled distributions, replica-resolved distributions,
raw angles, and circular statistics:
  /work1/ted/June2023/WithLigand/analysis_replicas/ligand_dihedrals/Z944_vs_mZ944
