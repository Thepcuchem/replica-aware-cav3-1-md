Trajectory sampling:

  MM/GBSA calculations were analyzed.
  Reported frames per residue: 599

  Snapshot interval: 0.500 ns
  Protein residues evaluated per replica: 503

Method:
  The run1 pairwise residue-ligand protocol was reproduced. For each
  protein residue and frame:
    DECOMP_ELEC = ELECT(residue+DZR) - ELECT(residue) - ELECT(DZR)
    DECOMP_VDW = VDW(residue+DZR) - VDW(residue) - VDW(DZR)
    DECOMP_TOTAL = DECOMP_ELEC + DECOMP_VDW
  Energies are in kcal/mol. Negative values indicate favorable
  residue-ligand contributions. The reported SD is the temporal SD.
  This pairwise GBIS decomposition is not strictly additive; sums of
  residue values need not equal the whole-complex binding energy.
